# Copy checklist

Every skill runs this list on its own output before saving. `/review` runs it on anything the user hands it.
Fix what fails. Do not hand over copy that fails a check in section 1 or 2.

## 1. Truth (blocking)

- [ ] Every number, result, testimonial, client name, review score and statistic appears in
      `business/05_PROOF.md`. Anything else is removed or replaced with `[ADD PROOF: ...]`.
- [ ] No promise of results. A guarantee is only mentioned if it is in `business/04_OFFER.md`, and it is
      quoted exactly, never made stronger.
- [ ] No fake urgency or scarcity. Deadlines, limited spots and price increases are only mentioned if real.
- [ ] No claim about competitors or "the market" that cannot be proven.
- [ ] Prices match `business/04_OFFER.md` exactly.

## 2. Rules (blocking)

- [ ] Nothing breaks `business/06_RULES.md` (check the "Never say" list word by word).
- [ ] Ads do not call out a personal attribute of the viewer (health, money problems, age, body, beliefs,
      sexuality). "Are you drowning in debt?" fails. "A plan for paying off debt" passes.
- [ ] Regulated topics (health, finance, income claims, legal) are handled the way `06_RULES.md` says.

## 3. Voice

- [ ] Written in the language set in `business/01_BUSINESS.md`.
- [ ] Uses the reader address from `business/03_VOICE.md` the whole way through, never mixed.
- [ ] None of the words from "Words we never use" in `03_VOICE.md`.
- [ ] Read it next to the writing samples in `03_VOICE.md`. If it sounds like a different person, rewrite.
- [ ] Uses the customer's own words from the language bank in `02_AUDIENCE.md` where they fit.

## 4. Sounds human

- [ ] No em dashes or en dashes used as punctuation. Use a period or a comma.
- [ ] No hype or filler. Remove: unlock, elevate, game-changer, revolutionize, supercharge, seamless,
      cutting-edge, leverage, delve, dive into, navigate (as in "navigate the complexities"), landscape,
      realm, tapestry, testament, journey (unless literal), empower, transform (unless concrete),
      "in today's fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y", "whether you're X or Y",
      "look no further", "take your business to the next level", "the secret to", "here's the thing".
- [ ] No three adjectives in a row. No rhetorical question followed by its own answer as a pattern.
- [ ] Specific beats vague. "Profit per job by the 10th" beats "better financial clarity".
- [ ] Sentence length varies. Some short. Most under 20 words. None over 30.
- [ ] Exclamation marks: zero in body copy, at most one in a whole page.

## 5. Selling

- [ ] The first line earns the second. Headline or hook is specific to this audience and this offer.
      If it could be used by a competitor without changes, rewrite it.
- [ ] Benefits are concrete outcomes, not features. Each claim passes "so what?".
- [ ] Matches the reader's awareness level in `02_AUDIENCE.md` (unaware readers need the problem first,
      product aware readers need the offer and proof first).
- [ ] One clear call to action, the one in `business/04_OFFER.md`, said the same way every time.
      Exception: early emails in a welcome sequence may ask for a small step (open the freebie, reply)
      before the main call to action.
- [ ] The biggest objection from `02_AUDIENCE.md` is answered somewhere.
- [ ] Headlines, hooks and ad copy come in 3 to 5 variants with the angle labeled, and no more than two
      variants use the same angle. Exception: a hook bank (15 to 20 hooks) or a set of Google Search
      headlines, which spreads across at least 4 angles with no angle used more than 4 times.

## 6. Delivery

- [ ] Saved to `output/<type>/YYYY-MM-DD_short-name.md` (or `.html`).
- [ ] Every placeholder is in square brackets and listed for the user at the end.
