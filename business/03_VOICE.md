# Voice

How the business sounds, and how it looks. Every piece of copy is matched against this file so it sounds like
you and not like generic AI. The examples are from a made-up company.

## Tone

Two to four words that describe how the writing should feel, plus one sentence on what that means in practice.

Example: "Direct, practical, a little dry. Talks like a bookkeeper who used to work on job sites, not like a
bank."

[Your tone]

## How we address the reader

One of: "you", "we and you", or neutral. Pick one and stick to it.

Example: "you"

[Your choice]

## Words we use

Words and phrases that are typical for you, including industry terms.

Example: job costing, profit per job, retainage, "the books", "know your numbers"

[Your words]

## Words we never use

Words that feel fake or wrong for your brand.

Example: synergy, solutions, world-class, game-changer, "financial wellness"

[Words you never use]

## Reference voices

People or brands whose way of writing you like. This points the voice in the right direction.

Example: "Basecamp's blog. Plain, opinionated, short."

[Your reference voices]

## Writing samples

Three to five sentences you wrote yourself and like, copied word for word. This is the most valuable part of
the file, because it is the only thing the finished copy can be compared against.

Example:
> "Most contractors find out if a job made money four months after it's done. That's too late to fix anything.
> We send you profit per job by the 10th, every month."

[Your own sentences, word for word]
