# Audience

Who you sell to, what bothers them, what they want, and the words they use. Every piece of copy is aimed at
the person described here. The examples are from a made-up company.

## Dream customer

Who they are (role, age range, situation, income or company size) and how aware they are of the problem.
Awareness levels: unaware, problem aware, solution aware, product aware, most aware.

Example: "Owner of a construction company with 10 to 30 employees, 35 to 55 years old, started as a tradesman.
Does the books at night or has a part-time bookkeeper who is behind. Problem aware. Knows the numbers are a
mess, has not looked for a specialist."

[Your dream customer]

## Pains

What frustrates or worries them today. Use their own words where you can, from calls, emails or reviews.

Example:
- "I don't know if I made money on a job until my accountant tells me in April."
- "My bookkeeper is three months behind and I'm scared to ask why."
- "I keep bidding jobs blind."

[Their pains, in their words]

## Dream outcome

What life looks like after the problem is solved. Concrete and specific.

Example: "They know their profit per job every month, bid with confidence, and stop doing books on Sunday
night."

[Their dream outcome]

## What they have tried

What they used before and why it did not work.

Example: "QuickBooks on their own (no time), their spouse (strain on the marriage), a general bookkeeper
(doesn't understand job costing)."

[What they tried before]

## What holds them back

Two things stop people from buying even when the pain is real.

Inertia, why they put it off. Example: "It's tax season, I'll deal with it after."
[Their inertia]

Fears and objections, what they worry will happen if they buy. Example: "Switching will make an even bigger
mess." "It's too expensive for a company my size."
[Their fears and objections]

## Language bank

Exact quotes from real customers. Never paraphrase. These become hooks, headlines and FAQ answers.

| Quote | Source | Category |
|---|---|---|
| "I keep bidding jobs blind." | Sales call | Pain |
| "I just want to know which jobs make money." | Email | Desired outcome |
| "Won't switching make a bigger mess?" | Sales call | Objection |

Categories: Pain, Desired outcome, Objection, Trigger moment, Success.

[Your quotes]

## Best customer ever

Your best customer, their situation before, the result, and why they were the best. This is the avatar all
copy is written for.

Example: "Mike, a roofer with 14 employees. Before: books 4 months behind. After 3 months: knew his margin per
job and dropped two job types that lost money."

[Your best customer]
