# Proof

The only allowed source of proof. Copy may only use numbers, results, testimonials and names that are listed
here. If something is not in this file, it does not go in the copy. The examples are from a made-up company.

## Testimonials

Real quotes, word for word, with name, context, and whether you have permission to use the name publicly.

Example:
> "I found out two of my job types lost money. We stopped bidding them. Best decision I made all year."
> Mike R., roofing company, 14 employees. Permission to use name: yes

[Your testimonials]

## Results and case studies

Before and after, with real numbers, and where the number comes from.

Example: "Roofing client: books went from 4 months behind to current in 6 weeks. Source: client file, 2025."

[Your results]

## Numbers

Facts that show volume, experience or quality, with a source and a date.

Example:
- 64 construction clients (client list, March 2026)
- 4.9 out of 5 on Google, 87 reviews (Google profile, March 2026)
- In business since 2017

[Your numbers]

## Credentials

Certifications, memberships, awards, press.

Example: "Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. Member of the Colorado Society of CPAs."
