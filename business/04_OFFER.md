# Offer

What you sell, what it costs, what the customer gets, and what you want people to do next. Be concrete.
The examples are from a made-up company.

## Main offer

Name, what is included, who it is for, and how and when it is delivered.

Example: "Brightline Monthly. Monthly bookkeeping, job costing report by the 10th, payroll, and a 30 minute
call each month to go through the numbers. For construction companies with 5 to 50 employees. Starts with a
2 week onboarding."

[Your main offer]

## Price

The price, or the range and what changes it. Say if you show the price publicly or only on a call.

Example: "From $890 a month, depending on number of employees and jobs. Price is shown on the website."

[Your price]

## Guarantee

The exact promise if something goes wrong. If you have no guarantee, write "None". Copy will never promise
more than what is written here.

Example: "If your job costing report is not in your inbox by the 10th, that month is free."

[Your guarantee, word for word, or None]

## Other offers

A cheaper first step, a premium option, or something free.

Example:
- Free: "Job Profit Checklist", a 1 page checklist (lead magnet)
- Entry: Year-end cleanup, from $2,500 one time
- Premium: Fractional CFO, from $3,500 a month

[Your other offers]

## Customer journey

What happens from first contact to result.

Example:
1. Book a 20 minute call
2. We look at your last 3 months of books
3. Proposal within 2 business days
4. Onboarding, 2 weeks
5. First job costing report by the 10th of the next month

[Your customer journey]

## Main call to action

The one thing you want people to do, and where it happens.

Example: "Book a free 20 minute books review at brightlinebooks.com/call"

[Your call to action and link]
