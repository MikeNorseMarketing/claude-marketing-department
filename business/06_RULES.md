# Rules

What your marketing must never say or promise, and what it always does. These rules win over everything else
in this folder. The examples are from a made-up company.

## Fixed rules (always on)

These apply to every business. Remove one only if you know exactly why.

- Never promise or guarantee results that are not guaranteed in `04_OFFER.md`.
- Never use a number, result, testimonial or name that is not in `05_PROOF.md`.
- Never name competitors in ads or copy. Comparative claims need proof and give them free attention.
- Never use fake urgency or fake scarcity. A deadline or a limit is only mentioned if it is real.
- In ads, never call out a personal attribute of the viewer (for example "Are you in debt?" or "Struggling
  with your weight?"). Ad platforms reject this.

## Legal and industry rules

Rules for your industry and market. Health, finance, legal, real estate, supplements and coaching on income
all have extra rules. Check your local consumer protection and advertising rules.

Example:
- We do not give tax advice in marketing. We say "talk to your CPA".
- Prices to consumers include tax where the law requires it.
