# Business

The basic facts about the business. Every skill reads this first. Fill it in with `/setup`, or by hand.
The examples below are from a made-up company and are only there to show the level of detail.

## Company

Name, website, where you operate, and one sentence on what you do.

Example: "Brightline Bookkeeping, brightlinebooks.com, Denver, Colorado. A bookkeeping firm with 6 people that
does monthly books and payroll for small construction companies."

[Your company]

## What we sell

A short overview of the main products or services. The details go in `04_OFFER.md`.

Example:
- Monthly bookkeeping for construction companies with 5 to 50 employees
- Payroll and contractor payments
- Year-end cleanup for companies that are behind

[Your products or services]

## Positioning

Where you sit in the market. Segment (premium, mid, budget), specialty, and who you do NOT want as a customer.

Example: "Mid to premium. We only work with construction, so we know job costing and retainage. We do not
take restaurants, retail or companies under 5 employees."

[Your positioning]

## Origin story

Why this business exists. The real event or insight, not "we saw a gap in the market".

Example: "Our founder did the books for her father's roofing company and watched him almost go under because
nobody tracked job costs. She started Brightline so contractors know which jobs make money."

[Your story]

## What makes us different

What you do that the alternatives do not. Concrete, not "great service".

Example: "Job costing on every project, every month. Our clients see profit per job by the 10th of the next
month. Most bookkeepers only deliver a profit and loss for the whole company."

[What makes you different]

## Language and market

The language every deliverable is written in, the country or region you sell in, and the currency.

Example: "English (US). United States, mostly Colorado. USD."

[Your language, market and currency]
